Act · Margdarshak मार्गदर्शक Available now

The right nudge, to the right manager, with a holdout that proves it

A hypothesis in plain English resolves to a precise cohort from behavioural signals. Python decides who is eligible and who is held back; the model writes one short, grounded sentence. At the readout date the two arms are compared and the lift is reported with a confidence interval — or withheld, if fewer than three people would make it identifiable.
