Connect · Sangam सङ्गम Included with every edition

One roster, one consent ledger, every system

Sangam reads your HRMS, ATS, listening, recognition and file drops into one governed model, resolves every employee to one identity, and records the lawful basis for each purpose. The three agents subscribe to it; nobody imports a CSV twice.

Request a demoPricing
  • Library connectors: Darwinbox, SuccessFactors, Keka, TurboHire, RippleHire, SmartRecruiters, inFeedo, Advantage Club, Klaar; plus CSV, SFTP, S3 and read-only SQL
  • A masked table profile — never a raw row — goes to an in-VPC model that proposes the mapping; your data owner approves it; ingestion is deterministic
  • Joiner, mover and leaver events computed once and pushed to every product
  • Erase a person once; every product acknowledges; evidence exportable
  • Lineage on every number: which system, which run, when; data health per source

Sangam is included with every edition from the November 2026 platform release. Its connector library already runs for our existing customers; the console, feeds and consent ledger ship with the release.
