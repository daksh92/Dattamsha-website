How we work · Ninety days

Ninety days from read access to a read-out in rupees

Five steps. At each one, what we do and the little you give. By Day 90 the Sunday-night case file is routine.

  1. Weeks 1–2

    Connect

    What we do

    Sangam joins your HRMS (Darwinbox, Keka, SAP SuccessFactors, Zoho People), ATS, LMS and survey tools into one governed employee record in your cloud. Nightly pipelines in Airflow; Soda checks on every load.

    What you give

    Read access to each system, one data owner, and a cloud account in your name. About four hours of your team’s time.

  2. Weeks 3–5

    Define

    What we do

    We sit with HR and Finance and sign off the metric definitions: what counts as voluntary attrition, which headcount is the denominator, what is withheld. 58 ISO 30414 metrics come predefined; you change the ones that must match how you run.

    What you give

    Two hours a week from an HR lead and one from Finance. Decisions, not documents.

  3. Weeks 6–8

    Switch on

    What we do

    Gargi, Birbal and Sutradhar go live inside your cloud. Sutradhar reads your policies; Birbal starts the nightly checks; the first Monday brief goes out to a pilot zone.

    What you give

    Your policy documents, the names of approvers, and a pilot zone or business unit.

  4. Weeks 9–12

    Act

    What we do

    Margdarshak sends the first nudges, each approved by a named person and measured against teams that were not nudged. We run the weekly cadence with your HRBPs.

    What you give

    HRBPs who approve or decline each nudge in under a minute. We handle the rest.

  5. Day 90

    Read out

    What we do

    Phala’s first value statement: what moved, what it was worth in rupees, what was not measured and why. Then we agree the next quarter.

    What you give

    Ninety minutes with the CHRO and the CFO.

The team · Who runs it

A people analytics function is people, agents and a cadence

Our analysts

Two people-analytics analysts on your account, who have run these functions inside Indian enterprises. They write the brief, sit in your business reviews, and own the numbers in front of Finance.

Our agents

Birbal checks 58 metrics across every team each night. Gargi answers questions in plain English. Sutradhar answers employees. Margdarshak sends what a person approved.

Your people

An HR lead who decides, HRBPs who approve, and a data owner who grants access. No one on your side writes SQL or builds a dashboard.

The week · Cadence

What a normal week looks like once it runs

Sunday night
Birbal runs every metric for every team and opens cases on what moved.
Monday 7am
The Monday brief reaches each HRBP in app, on Slack or by email: what needs approval, what is being watched, what worked.
Tuesday
Nudges that were approved go out to managers on WhatsApp or Teams, with a holdout set aside.
Thursday
Our analyst joins your business review with the numbers already agreed with Finance.
Quarter end
Phala closes the quarter with a value statement in rupees.
