Why we exist · The gap

HR systems already know. Nobody had made them answer, act and prove it.

Every enterprise we worked in had the data and none of them had the function: the people, the definitions and the cadence that turn a Monday question into a Tuesday action. Buying a dashboard did not fix it. Hiring a team took a year. So we built the function as something you can switch on, inside your own walls, in Indian colour and with Indian names.

The names · Why Sanskrit

Because a name that means something is remembered, and a name that describes the job is honest

Every enterprise tool is called something like Insights Hub or People Cloud, and nobody remembers which does what. We are an Indian company building for Indian enterprises, and India has a vocabulary for exactly these roles: the questioner, the narrator, the guide, the fruit. A manager who gets a Monday note from Birbal knows it came from the analyst; a nudge from Margdarshak is a guide pointing the way. The names describe the job, and the job has to live up to the name.
