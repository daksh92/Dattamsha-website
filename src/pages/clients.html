Telecom · India

The employee relationship management tool

The question
Managers and HRBPs assembled a picture of each team by hand from the HRMS, the LMS, the survey tool and their own notes before every conversation.
What we built
Sangam as the data layer, then one view per team for managers and HRBPs: tenure, movement, learning, engagement and open items in one place. Dashboards, predictive models, a newsletter and nudges on the same layer.
What runs today
The fullest Anvaya deployment today.

Quick commerce · frontline · India

Text-to-insights for frontline teams

The question
Zonal and city leaders waited on a central analytics queue for every people question, and read the answer in a deck days later.
What we built
Gargi for plain-English questions on governed metrics, Newsletter Studio for the weekly brief, and the nudge engine for frontline managers. Business scorecards for every frontline team on the same technology.
What runs today
Dattamsha’s largest deployment by headcount.

Fintech · India

The compensation revision cycle, end to end

The question
The annual comp cycle lived in spreadsheets: collecting data from managers, chasing approvals, and generating hundreds of letters by hand.
What we built
The total rewards tool: data collection, manager workflow and approvals, and generated letters in one application. Also sales incentive automation, dashboarding, a survey tool, and Sutradhar as AskHR.
What runs today
Custom applications on the Anvaya layer.

Internet services · frontline · India

An AI-enabled onboarding application

The question
New joiners between offer release and Day 90 dropped out or arrived without documents, and HR Ops chased each one by phone.
What we built
A custom onboarding application from offer release to Day 90: candidate Q&A, document collection with verification through Setu APIs, introductions, and handoff to the HRBP. Human in the loop at every step.
What runs today
Identity checks through a regulated API; Aadhaar and PAN never sit in the system.

Healthcare · India

Employee surveys, end to end

The question
Surveys went out, results came back as a deck, and nothing visibly changed, so response rates fell each round.
What we built
The whole loop: surveys designed on behavioural-science principles, administered, analysed, insights delivered to each manager, and actions tracked to closure.
What runs today
Listening to action in a single engagement.
