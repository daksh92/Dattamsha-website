Privacy notice · For visitors to this website

What this website collects, and why

This notice stands on its own. It covers dattamsha.co.in only; deployments of Anvaya inside a client’s cloud are covered by that client’s notice to its employees.

Who we are
Dattamsha Data Labs Private Limited, Jammu, is the data fiduciary for this website. Contact: daksh@dattamsha.co.in.
What we collect
Only what you send us: the name, email and time you enter when you book a session, or the email your mail app sends when you leave a question. No cookies. No analytics.
Why
To arrange and prepare the working session you asked for. No other purpose, no marketing lists.
How long
Ninety days after the session, or ninety days after your message if no session takes place. Then deleted.
Who else
Two providers handle it for us: Google, which runs our email and the booking calendar, and GitHub, which hosts these pages and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. Their servers may be outside India. No one else, and never for marketing.
Your rights
Ask what we hold, correct it, or have it erased by writing to daksh@dattamsha.co.in. We answer within 30 days. Withdrawing is as easy as giving: reply to any email from us.
Grievances
Grievance officer: Daksh Sahni, daksh@dattamsha.co.in. If unresolved, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.
