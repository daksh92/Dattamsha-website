Home · About

Built by someone who has been the buyer

Dattamsha Data Labs was founded by Daksh Sahni after a decade running HR analytics, compensation and HR digitisation at Pine Labs, Swiggy, Bharti Airtel, Sun Life and Concentrix. The company is profitable and self-funded, builds on open-source infrastructure, and keeps employee data in-region.

Open source first

Postgres, Qdrant, Airflow-class orchestration, open models where they meet the bar. Cost-effective, inspectable, and never a licence your procurement team has to fight.

Compliance by construction

Lawful basis, consent, retention and erasure are properties of the platform, not a programme run beside it. If a product will not show a number it cannot support, the buyer can trust the numbers it does show.

Python decides, the model writes

Every decision that touches a person is deterministic and auditable. Models propose mappings and write sentences; they never select, compute or disclose.
