Why a loop

Each HR vendor solves the part it can see

The HRMS adds "AI"

It answers questions about its own tables. It cannot join your ATS to your exits, or your helpdesk to your engagement survey — and organisations run six systems for good reasons.

The analytics suite stops at the chart

Global people-analytics platforms price from $50–100K a year, take a quarter to implement, and end at a dashboard. Nobody is nudged; nothing is measured.

The survey tool bolts on a nudge

Action without measurement does not survive as a category. A nudge you cannot prove changed anything is a notification.

The durable position is the one none of them can take: cross-system, decision-to-action, with the outcome measured.

Compliance by construction

Three data-protection clocks, one ledger

India · DPDP Act

Full enforcement 13 May 2027

Consent Manager registration opens 13 November 2026. Penalties to ₹250 Cr. Every employer needs a lawful basis for every employee record.

India →
Malaysia · PDPA

DPO and breach notification mandatory since June 2025

Employee data processed for stated purposes; a named data protection officer; breaches notified to the Commissioner.

Malaysia →
UAE · PDPL

Full enforcement 2026

Lawful basis, cross-border rules and data-subject rights, with penalties to AED 5 M. Saudi Arabia's PDPL is already issuing decisions.

UAE →

Lawful basis per person per purpose, consent per channel and notice version, retention enforced nightly, one erasure across every product, and an exportable evidence pack. What we can and cannot claim today →
